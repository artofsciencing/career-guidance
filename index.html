Trend 1 — AI & Automation
The age of intelligent automation is already here
AI is actively reshaping every sector from manufacturing to finance. By 2031, an estimated 85 million roles will be augmented — but the same wave creates 97 million new roles requiring human–AI collaboration skills.

Key Insights

Prompt engineering is the new baseline skill for every profession
Agentic AI systems will automate multi-step knowledge work by 2027
AI-native companies hold a 34% productivity advantage
Highest growth: AI trainers, automation auditors, workflow designers

Trend 2 — Green & Sustainable Careers
The green economy is the fastest-growing sector on earth
$9.2 trillion in global investment flowing into clean energy and sustainable infrastructure by 2030. This creates a talent shortage unlike any other — 24 million new green jobs with not enough trained workers to fill them.

Key Insights

Renewable energy technicians are the fastest-growing trade qualification in the UK & EU
ESG reporting is becoming mandatory for FTSE 350 companies
Green building specialists earn 28% higher salaries on average
Circular economy designers are in critical shortage across Europe

Trend 3 — Remote & Digital Healthcare
Healthcare is going digital at an unstoppable pace
The global digital health market is projected to reach $1.3 trillion by 2031. AI diagnostics, wearable health tech, and precision medicine are creating entirely new professional categories that didn't exist five years ago.

Key Insights

Telehealth physicians earn up to 40% more than in-person-only practitioners
Digital health data analysts are among the top 5 NHS sought-after profiles
Mental health app usage has grown 500% since 2020
Health informatics bridges clinical care and technology systems
